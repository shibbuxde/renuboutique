Can I get my own fabric stitched here?

Yes — bridal lehengas, party gowns, festive suits and family outfits for weddings and functions. For weddings, we suggest coming in early so fittings can be done comfortably.

Do you also do alterations and finishing work?

Yes — fitting alterations, fall and pico, lace and border attachment and other finishing work. Bring your outfit along and we will take care of it.
